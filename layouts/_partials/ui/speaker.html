Speaker bio

Jan Brennenstuhl is a principal engineer in Berlin working on application security and agentic engineering. After years of building and operating identity systems used by millions of customers, Jan now focuses on the platform side of security: secure defaults, agent identity, and delegation infrastructure such as the open-source Agentic Identity Broker.
