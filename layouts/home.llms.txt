# {{ site.Title }}

> Essays, a field guide and research notes by Jan Brennenstuhl on application security, identity, passwords, tokens and agentic engineering. Every page has a Markdown twin at its URL plus `index.md`, and every claim in the field guide has a stable anchor you can cite.

Jan Brennenstuhl is a principal engineer in Berlin working on application security and agent identity. Original writing is CC BY 4.0 unless an entry says otherwise: reuse it, and link the canonical URL. Publication, modification and review dates mean different things; a rebuild is not a review.

## How to read this site

- [Full corpus as one Markdown file]({{ "llms-full.txt" | absURL }}): every entry below, newest first.
- [Catalog]({{ "catalog.json" | absURL }}): IDs, URLs, dates, review status, topics, license and content hashes for every entry.
- [Findings]({{ "findings.json" | absURL }}): every quotable finding with its anchor URL and source.
- [JSON Feed]({{ "feed.json" | absURL }}) and [RSS]({{ "index.xml" | absURL }}): full-content updates. Each topic has its own feeds.
- [Glossary]({{ "glossary/" | absURL }}): definitions of OAuth, OIDC, JOSE and agent-identity terms, with anchors.
- Content negotiation: request any page with `Accept: text/markdown` to get its Markdown twin.
- [capabilities.txt]({{ "capabilities.txt" | absURL }}): what an agent can do here, including the read-only MCP server at https://mcp.janbrennenstuhl.eu/mcp.
