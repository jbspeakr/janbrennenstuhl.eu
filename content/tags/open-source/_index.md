Have you ever wondered what drives many technological innovations and advancements today? What fuels your favorite apps, technology, and, most crucially, the internet? The power of Open Source Software (OSS) holds the answer. Open Source Software, is the foundation of many technology around us. It is a software development approach that promotes accessibility and collaborative enhancement, resulting in increased transparency, reliability, and faster evolution.
