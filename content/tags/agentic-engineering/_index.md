Agentic engineering replaces the traditional write-review-merge cycle with fast, autonomous loops: coding agents plan, implement, run tests, and verify their own output with minimal human handoff. The implications reach across the entire engineering discipline — how teams build tooling, how organizations scale verification, and how the roles of individual engineers evolve. The following articles examine what this shift demands and how to build the foundation to work with it effectively.
