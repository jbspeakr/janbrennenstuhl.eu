Have you ever wondered why, despite advances in cyber security technologies, data breaches and frauds continue to proliferate? It may surprise you that it is typically a simple, well-executed scam that causes the most damage, rather than sophisticated programming or complex malware. Hello, and welcome to the world of social engineering. In essence, social engineering is a non-technical method used by cyber criminals to trick people into disclosing critical information. They take advantage of human psychology, which is a flaw in any organization.
