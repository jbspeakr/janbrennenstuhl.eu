Have you ever questioned the security of your digital assets, personal information, or online transactions? With the increase of cybercrime, data breaches, and identity theft, ensuring the integrity and security of our online interactions has never been more important. But what if we told you there's a solution that can boost security while also making our lives easier - passwordless authentication? Passwordless authentication is a method that confirms a user's identification without the need for the user to memorize and type complex passwords.
