## Reading and search

This site sets no cookies. Search runs in your browser after downloading a static index. Search terms are not sent to an analytics service. A query in the address bar is part of the page URL requested from the hosting service.

## Page-view counting

The production site loads one script from [Plausible](https://plausible.io/data-policy), an EU-hosted analytics service. It counts page views and referrers without cookies, without a persistent identifier and without storing your IP address; the data is aggregated and cannot be traced to a person. Browsers that block the script lose nothing: every page works without it.

## Color preference

If you choose a light or dark theme, the site saves that preference in your browser’s local storage. Choosing System removes it. The preference is not sent to the server. If storage is unavailable, reading and theme selection still work.

## Hosting and external links

The site is delivered through Bunny. HTTP requests necessarily include connection information such as your IP address; this page does not make claims about hosting-provider log retention. External links, including video and presentation links, open the respective provider’s site.
