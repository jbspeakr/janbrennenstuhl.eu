I work in Berlin as a principal engineer, focused on application security and on making agentic engineering more secure and efficient.

## Building

Most of my time goes into the [Agentic Identity Broker]({{< ref "/projects/agentic-identity-broker" >}}): delegation and consent infrastructure, so that an agent can act for a person without holding that person's credentials. The [retrospective]({{< ref "/post/agentic-identity-broker" >}}) covers what 10 months of building it with coding agents taught me.

## Writing

I am turning the knowledge base into a [field guide]({{< ref "/knowledge-base" >}}): short, citable answers with the evidence behind them, starting with password reuse and the new [agent identity]({{< ref "/knowledge-base/agent-identity" >}}) area.
