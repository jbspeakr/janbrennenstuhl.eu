Hi. My name is Jan Brennenstuhl, I live and work in Berlin Germany, where I was born and raised. I am a product-minded software engineer, web security enthusiast & clean code artist with a huge interest in interdisciplinary software development and engineering leadership. I am a big fan of cycling sports, coffee & <a href="https://www.rucksackrebellen.de" title="Rucksackrebellen Wander-Blog">hiking</a>.

I currently work in Berlin as principal engineer, mainly balancing security with friction for our customers. I modernize customer identity and access management solutions and engineer single sign-on (SSO) solutions.

I've worked as software engineer and lead on e-commerce funnels, rule engines, cloud strategies, IAM and SSO solutions, REST APIs, system integrations, technology research, legacy migrations, decommissioning projects and modularizing monolithic systems - various things, mostly in e-commerce and security though. I practiced software archaeology more than I set up greenfield web services.   

I am an internet standards enthusiast, who values open source software and open formats over custom builds. For me, quality software products are created best by small teams of product-minded engineers with a narrow, well-defined business scope and a clear vision. I'm especially passionate about leading engineers as well as creating and operating reliable, secure and maintainable software products.

I hold a M.Sc. in Computer Science from the **University of Potsdam**, where I completed my thesis on *"Certificate Management in the Cloud for Authentication in university Environment"*. I’ve also studied Media Informatics at the **Beuth University** of Applied Sciences in Berlin.
